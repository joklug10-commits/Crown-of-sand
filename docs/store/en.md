**Description:**
Your plot of land lies deep in the desert. Pick a castle site, raise the keep and turn a few tents into a fortress that can withstand any assault.

BUILD
• Woodcutters, quarries, farms, mill and bakery: every good travels from raw material to your stockpile.
• Taxes and rations decide whether your people stay loyal.
• Walls, towers, moats and pitch: plan your defense piece by piece.

DEFEND
• The Desert Lord in the northeast sends ever stronger armies.
• Archers, spearmen, swordsmen and knights hold the walls.
• Siege engines, ladders and fire attacks demand quick decisions.

CONQUER
• A campaign with 5 missions, or free play on random maps.
• Three difficulty levels and adjustable opponents.
• Goal: destroy the enemy keep or survive ten attacks.

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
• Touch controls with gestures to pan, zoom and rotate.
• A dedicated iPhone layout for landscape play.
• Save locally, back up saves as a file and continue on another device.
• English and German.

FAIR
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.
• No data collection. Everything stays on your device.
